About Us

Screen To Street is an independent guide created to help fans find, in the real world, the places visited, photographed, or filmed by their favorite artists: show sets, restaurants, museums, concert halls, or music video locations.

The site lists public places and information already shared publicly (broadcasts, social networks, press), organized in the form of interactive maps by artist, category, and city, with an itinerary generator to prepare a trip following the footsteps of your favorite group.

Screen To Street is an independent project and is not affiliated, associated, or endorsed by any agency, record label, artist, or right holder mentioned on the site. All artist names, group names, and trademarks mentioned remain the property of their respective owners.
